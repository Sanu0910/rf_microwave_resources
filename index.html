Open-Source RF & EM Simulation Tools

OpenEMS

FDTD solver with MATLAB/Python scripting. Great for 3D EM simulation.

Elmer FEM

Multiphysics FEM solver with a GUI — handles coupled EM and thermal problems.

Scikit-RF

Python toolkit for RF/microwave engineering — S-parameter analysis, network design.

MEEP

FDTD solver in Python/Scheme/C++ — powerful for photonics and antenna simulation.

SciLab

Open-source MATLAB alternative for numerical computation and signal processing.

FreeFEM

FEM solver for complex multiphysics PDEs — good for custom EM formulations.

NEC

Method of Moments solver for wire and surface antenna simulation.

FEniCS

High-level FEM platform for PDE solving in Python/C++ — research-grade.

Puma-EM

Surface MoM solver using Multilevel Fast Multipole Method (MLFMM).

Traceon

Electron optics simulator using BEM and particle tracing techniques.

QucsStudio

2D circuit and EM simulator — fast for S-parameter and filter design.

PSSFSS

Design and analysis tool for Frequency Selective Surfaces (FSS) — Julia-based.

KiCAD

Professional PCB design suite with built-in SPICE simulation.

GNU Octave

Powerful open-source MATLAB alternative — runs most MATLAB code without changes.
