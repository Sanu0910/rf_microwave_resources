Open Source Tools for RF & EM Simulation

OpenEMS

FDTP solver with support for differential equations. Supports MATLAB and Python scripting.

Elmer FEM

Finite Element Solver with GUI for multiphysics simulations.

Scikit-RF

Python-based RF and microwave simulation toolkit.

MEEP

FDTP solver with scripting support in Python, Scheme, or C++.

SciLab

Open-source software for numerical computation, an alternative to MATLAB.

FreeFEM

Finite Element Method (FEM) solver for complex multiphysics simulations.

NEC

Method of Moments (MoM) solver for antenna simulation on wire and surface structures.

FEniCS

FEM platform for solving partial differential equations in Python/C++.

Puma-EM

Surface MoM solver using Multilevel Fast Multipole Method (MLFMM).

Traceon

Electron optics simulator using BEM and particle tracing techniques.

QUC Studio

2D circuit and EM simulator supporting various RF components.

PSSFSS

Tool for design and analysis of Frequency Selective Surfaces (FSS).

KiCAD

PCB design suite with built-in SPICE simulator for circuit analysis.

Octave

Powerful open-source alternative to MATLAB for numerical analysis.
